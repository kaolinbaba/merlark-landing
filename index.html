Channel monitoring for YouTube creators · Private beta

YouTube reviews your whole channel. Merlark keeps watch.

Merlark watches the content patterns across your channel and shows what changed, which videos produced each observation, and the timestamps behind it, so your next creative decision starts from evidence.

How it works

From connection to outcome, in five steps

  1. 01

    Connect a channel

    Google sign-in with read-only access. Further permissions only when a feature needs them.

  2. 02

    Inspect findings

    Patterns compared with your channel’s own history, marked working, slipping, neutral, emerging or insufficient evidence.

  3. 03

    Explore the evidence

    Every finding opens the specific videos and timestamps behind it. No evidence, no finding.

  4. 04

    Choose an intervention

    Decide what to try next, or draft a script grounded in your own findings.

  5. 05

    Track outcomes

    Later readings and your own notes are recorded separately, so Merlark never grades its advice with its own signal.

The product

Built from the same screens as the demo

Reconstructed history, clearly separated

When a channel connects, Merlark rebuilds its past from what is still accessible and draws a visible line at the moment of connection. Left of the line is a historical estimate; right of it is what Merlark observed.

See monitoring and backfill

Observation first, interpretation second

What was measured and what it might mean are always shown apart. A finding describes an association with your own baseline, never a cause, and says plainly when evidence is insufficient.

Open the findings

A script draft that cites its sources

Pick findings, inspect their sources, reorder the outline and export it. Evidence-backed lines keep their citation; structure and weak ideas are labelled as such.

How it is built

Principles

  • Official APIs onlyNo scraping and no unofficial data sources.
  • Least privilegeRead-only access, and additional permissions only when a feature that needs them is turned on.
  • Observation, not predictionNo risk scores, no probabilities, no verdicts. Merlark never says anything about a channel’s monetization status.
  • Built with ClaudeMerlark is developed with Claude Code. Using the Claude API inside the product is planned and not yet built.

Where it stands today

An honest split of what exists

Merlark is built by a solo founder and is being tested privately. The demo shows the full design with sample data; this is what is real.

Built, in private testing

  • Google sign-in with a read-only YouTube connection
  • Reading channel and video data through the official YouTube APIs
  • Automatic deletion of stored YouTube statistics within 30 days
  • Disconnecting a channel deletes its stored data

In development

  • Evidence-backed findings with video and timestamp citations
  • A visible line between reconstructed history and what Merlark has observed since connection
  • The weekly creator brief
  • Self-service account deletion

Planned

  • Script suggestions grounded only in a creator’s own stored findings
  • Using the Claude API to write reports from stored evidence
  • Paid Early Access plan with one-click cancellation

Long-horizon baselines, finding states and comparisons are derived metrics that YouTube’s developer policies allow only after an API audit. Merlark has not received that approval; today it keeps YouTube statistics for no more than 30 days.
