How it works

Five steps, one channel, your own history

  1. 01

    Monitor

    Connect with read-only Google sign-in. Merlark records each upload and its first-week reading.

  2. 02

    Find patterns

    Uploads that share a structure are grouped and compared with the rest of your channel.

  3. 03

    Inspect evidence

    Every finding opens the exact videos and timestamps behind it. No evidence, no finding.

  4. 04

    Compare

    Groups are measured against your own channel’s baseline, never someone else’s.

  5. 05

    Track outcomes

    Choose a change to try. Later readings join the same timeline, recorded separately from the finding.
