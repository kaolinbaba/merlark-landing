What’s available right now?

In private testing

  • Google sign-in with read-only YouTube access.
  • Channel and video data from the official YouTube APIs.
  • Stored YouTube statistics are deleted within 30 days.
  • Disconnecting a channel deletes its stored data.

Only in the demo

  • Patterns, comparisons, the weekly recap, experiments and drafts.
  • Months of channel history. The connected product keeps statistics for 30 days at most.
  • Everything runs on fictional data in your browser.

Waiting on approval

  • Patterns, comparisons and longer history count as derived metrics under YouTube’s developer policies. They need an API audit first, and Merlark hasn’t received that approval yet.
  • Merlark doesn’t estimate monetization status or predict YouTube decisions.
