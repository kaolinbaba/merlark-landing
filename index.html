How it works

Four buttons. That’s the whole idea.

Eight videos, one shared trick.

On our sample channel, 8 videos open with an animated map in the first ten seconds. Merlark noticed.

Every finding comes with receipts.

Tap a finding and jump straight to the video and the second it’s based on. No receipt, no finding.

Measured against you, not strangers.

Those eight averaged 29,450 views in week one. Your other long videos usually get 21,200. That’s 1.4× your usual. It’s a link, not proof of cause — and we’ll always say so.

The demo

Every screen, ready to poke around.

It all runs in your browser on a made-up channel. Nothing connects to Google, charges a card or calls an AI model.

  • FindingsYour patterns, sorted: working, slipping, emerging.
  • MonitoringWhat we rebuilt from your history vs. what we’ve watched since you connected.
  • VideosEvery upload, and what it’s evidence for.
  • CompareA group vs. your own usual. Too little data? We’ll tell you.
  • Weekly briefYour week, in one quick read.
  • ExperimentsPlan a tweak, then see how it went.
  • Script workspaceDrafts built on your findings, with the sources attached.

What’s real

No smoke, no mirrors.

Live in private testing

  • Google sign-in with read-only YouTube access
  • Channel and video data from the official YouTube APIs
  • Stored YouTube statistics deleted automatically within 30 days
  • Disconnecting a channel deletes its stored data

In the demo (sample data)

  • Findings with receipts, colours and your usual
  • Rebuilt history and the “you connected here” line
  • Weekly brief, comparisons, experiments
  • Script workspace built on your findings

Coming next

  • Claude API to write reports from stored evidence
  • Delete your account yourself, in one place
  • Paid Early Access with one-click cancellation

Findings, colours and comparisons count as “derived metrics” under YouTube’s developer policies, which allow them only after an API audit. Merlark hasn’t received that approval yet, and today it keeps YouTube statistics for 30 days at most. The months of history in the demo show the idea, not what runs today.
