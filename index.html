01 Discovery

Eight uploads share one opening.

On the fictional channel Lanternfish Histories, 8 long-form uploads open on an animated route map within the first ten seconds. Merlark groups uploads that share a structure and sets them against the rest of the channel.

WorkingEvidence: Strong

02 Evidence

Every finding points to a moment.

The Convoy That Sailed Into Fog, 0:00–0:10: Animated route map before narration.

Observed by Merlark on Aug 26, 2026, after the channel was connected. No evidence, no finding.

03 Comparison

Measured against the channel’s own baseline.

1.4×channel baseline

The map-first uploads averaged 29,450 views in their first week. The median of the other 20 long-form uploads is 21,200.

An association, not a cause: other differences between these videos could explain part of the gap. Earlier uploads are historical estimates reconstructed after connection; deleted, private or unavailable videos may not be represented.

04 Next step

A change worth trying, tracked on its own.

Try a route map in the first 10 seconds of the next travel-themed episode.

If you try it, later readings are recorded as outcomes, kept apart from the finding that suggested them.

The beta, screen by screen

One fictional channel, every screen of the product.

The demo runs entirely in your browser on fixed sample data. Nothing connects to Google, charges a card or calls an AI model.

  1. iFindingsEach pattern with its state, evidence quality, and observation kept apart from interpretation.

Where Merlark stands
